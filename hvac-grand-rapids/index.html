A typical week for our residential technicians might include capacitor and contactor replacements, flame sensor cleanings, refrigerant leak searches, blower motor swaps, plus the occasional full system replacement or ductless addition. How Grand Rapids Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
